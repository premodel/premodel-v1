FAQs

It really is that simple.

What should I expect from a Reveal?

Three photorealistic images of your room reimagined three different ways, based on what we hear from you at your home visit. They're a starting point: enough to know which direction excites you. A Plan is where we refine the details into a finished design.

Can I see more Reveal directions?

Yes. Beyond your three, we can create additional Reveals for a modest fee.

Where do you work?

We serve homeowners in the greater Seattle area today (King and Snohomish counties), and we're expanding region by region. Worth asking even if you're just outside.

How long does this take?

You get your Reveals in as little as 2 days after your home visit. New deliverables follow within 2 to 4 days of your feedback, so you lead the pace. We'll propose a full timeline alongside your Reveal, so you know what to expect before deciding whether to keep going.

How is Premodel different from a designer, architect, or design-build firm?

We're your first hire, and a lower-cost, lower-effort way to figure out what you want before you commit to anyone else. We help you determine what your project actually needs, then assemble the team (designer, architect, contractor) as part of your Plan.

Do I have to use your contractors?

No. Your Reveal and your Plan are yours to keep, and to take to whichever builder you choose. The three-bid intro is the optional last step of a Plan, never a requirement.
