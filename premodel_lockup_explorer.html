Signature lockup · tagline carries the accent.

The mark stays fully neutral. The "Premodel" wordmark stays deep ink. Only "Before You Remodel" — the tagline that is the positioning statement — takes the brand color. Five accents: two warm-family options (oxblood, saffron), two cool options (cyanotype, sage), and one purple wildcard (aubergine). The terracotta/rust pair was eliminated for sitting too close to Claude's coral. Toggle accents above; every lockup is a real PIL recolor of your source file.

Why this
  • Mark stays untouched and consistent forever
  • Wordmark keeps the gravitas of deep ink
  • Accent amplifies the value prop — color lives where the meaning is
  • If the accent ever evolves, only one element needs updating
Trade-offs
  • Brand color is more recessed than wordmark-accent or layer-accent

  • If the tagline ever gets dropped, the brand color disappears with it
