Editorial neutrals, applied.

The full color system for the new homepage. Every color in service of letting Reveal photography lead — restrained background, ink headlines, taupe supporting copy, one warm accent doing the work of the old navy. Hex values + CSS variable names included so this maps cleanly onto the prototype's existing token structure.

What changed.   Five accent candidates spanning the color wheel. Warm-family Claude-adjacent options (sienna, terracotta, rust) all retired.   ·   Oxblood (deep wine, default) and sage (PNW cool) carried forward.   ·   Cyanotype added — the architect's blueprint blue, with a direct conceptual tie to Premodel's value prop.   ·   Aubergine and saffron added as creative options outside the standard editorial-warm-neutral palette.   ·   Signature lockup approach: only the tagline carries the accent; mark and wordmark stay neutral. 01 · The palette

Surfaces

Page background
--bg-page
#f7f4eb
Warm paper. The body bg of the entire site — instead of #fff. Lets the cream layers of the mark blend into negative space and signals "editorial" before a single word is read.
Card / section surface
--bg-surface
#fbf8ef
Slightly lighter than page. For tiles, quote cards, How-to-Premodel steps. Establishes mild layering without resorting to drop shadows.
Pill / eyebrow background
--bg-pill
#ece4d1
A half-step darker than page. Eyebrow tags ("Why Premodel," "Get your estimate"), small icon chips, key callouts.
Dark surface
--bg-dark
#1a1816
Footer, dark hero overlays. Warm-toned near-black, never pure #000. When the logo lives here, it sits on a cream rounded card (see below).

Text

Primary text
--text-primary
#2a2826
Headlines, body copy. Warm-toned near-black to match the mark's deep-ink bottom layer — avoid pure #000 (cold, too SaaS).
Secondary text
--text-secondary
#6a625a
Supporting copy under headlines, lede paragraphs, tile descriptions. Warm taupe — same family as the mark's middle layer.
Tertiary text
--text-tertiary
#998f80
Eyebrow labels (when not on pill bg), captions, fine print, "Required" hints, metadata.
Text on dark
--text-on-dark
#f3ede1
When text sits on the dark surface (footer headlines, dark-hero overlay copy). Warm bone — same family as the mark's top translucent layer.

Borders, dividers, icons

Hairline divider
--border-divider
#e4dccb
Section breaks, table rows, between How-to-Premodel steps. Should be visible but quiet.
Subtle border
--border-subtle
#d8cfc0
Card outlines, input field borders, ghost buttons. The "second tier" of structure.
Strong border
--border-strong
#b8ae9c
Selected states, focused inputs, emphasized borders.
Icons (primary)
--icon-primary
#2a2826
Inline icons paired with text: arrows, checks, step numbers. Reads as part of the type.

Accent (currently: oxblood)

Accent — primary
--accent
#5e2c2e
The single brand accent. Lives in: CTAs, links, the tagline "Before You Remodel," key statistic numbers, quote marks. Replaces the navy.
Accent — hover/active
--accent-hover
#3f1c1d
Darker shade for hover and pressed states on accent surfaces.
Accent — soft tint
--accent-soft
#ecd0d0
Pale tint for accent-pill backgrounds, highlight blocks, callouts where the full accent would be too loud.
Accent — on dark
--accent-on-dark
#a4666a
Lightened shade for use on the dark surface. Same brand color, calibrated for contrast.

05 · Accent family · click a card to apply

Five candidates spanning the color wheel — three of them deliberately outside the editorial-warm-neutrals lane that's gotten crowded. Oxblood is the default; the others give you real range to react to. Every card is clickable; the entire page (palette swatches, mockup, lockup tagline) re-skins live.

Oxblood

#5e2c2e · #3f1c1d · #ecd0d0

Default. Deep wine — architectural, heritage, serious. Most distinctive of the warm options. Works at tagline scale where the deep saturation reads as deliberate.

· Default · active

Sage

#7d8a72 · #566052 · #e4ecd6

Cool PNW-coded. Quiet counterweight to the warm mark. Reads as "considered, calm, regional." Risk: sage has been peaking for ~3 years; trend-cycle dependent.

· active

Cyanotype · concept play

#2a5680 · #16365a · #c8d8e8

The architect's blueprint blue. Direct conceptual tie: Premodel literally shows you the blueprint of your home before you build it. Could own this the way Tiffany owns robin's egg.

· active

Aubergine

#4a2942 · #2e1828 · #d8c8d2

Deep plum. Sophisticated and unexpected — and quietly kitchen-coded (eggplant cabinetry is having a real moment in high-end interiors). Confident, individual, not following.

· active

Saffron

#c89028 · #966819 · #f4e0b8

Warm but yellow-leaning — completely different family than Claude's red-orange coral. Optimistic, generous, library-coded. Risk: pushed too far it reads "heritage brand."

· active
