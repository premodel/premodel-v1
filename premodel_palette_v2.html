03 Spacing scale

4-based scale with semantic step names.

Every gap, padding, and margin in the system comes from this 9-step scale. The bars below are sized to actual pixel value so you can eyeball the relationships.

Vertical rhythm guide
  • Within a card: headline → body uses --space-3 (12px). Body → CTA uses --space-5 (24px).
  • Between cards: always --space-4 (16px) on mobile, --space-5 (24px) on desktop.
  • Between sections: --space-8 (64px) on mobile, --space-9 (96px) on desktop.
  • Inside form fields: always --space-3 (12px) padding.
  • Page edge padding (gutter): --space-5 (24px) on mobile, --space-6 (32px) on desktop.

05 Iconography & imagery

Lucide line icons + Reveal photography only.

Inline icons stay subordinate to text. Photography earns its place — Reveal renders, real room photos, occasionally inspiration; never stock.

Icon sizing & stroke
16
18
24
32
48

Stroke: always 1.5px (matches Lucide default for editorial restraint).   ·   Color: --icon-primary for inline icons paired with text; --icon-muted for decorative-only.   ·   Accent icons live inside --accent-soft chips when an icon needs to pop, never as a standalone color choice.

Imagery rules
  • Reveal renders are the hero. They earn the carousel, the case-study modals, the social proof spots.
  • Real client room photos belong next to before/after explanations and on dedicated case-study pages.
  • Inspiration images are used sparingly and always attributed to the homeowner — never as a stand-in for our own work.
  • No stock photography. If we can't show our own work for a section, the section gets type and white space instead.
  • Aspect ratios: 4:3 for room interiors, 1:1 for avatars / detail crops, 16:9 for screen recordings of the VR walkthrough.
  • Corner radius: images inside cards use --radius-card (14px). Standalone editorial images stay un-rounded.
06 Voice & tone

Considered. Direct. Quietly confident.

Premodel sells calm. The voice mirrors the product — no urgency tricks, no jargon, no construction-industry posturing. We're advisors, not vendors.

Plainspoken

Short sentences. Specific nouns. No industry acronyms unless we define them in the same sentence. If a contractor would say it, we probably shouldn't.

Quietly confident

State the thing without hedging or oversell. "Photoreal renders of your actual space" beats "innovative AI-powered visualization solutions."

On the homeowner's side

Address the reader's actual experience — fragments of ideas, Pinterest fatigue, fear of committing — rather than describing our process from our perspective.

Terminology
Reveal
Always capitalized. It's the proper noun for our deliverable — photoreal renders + walkthrough. Lowercase "reveal" only as a generic verb.
Premodel
Can be used as a verb ("Premodel before you remodel") and as the company name. Both are intentional. Always one word, capital P.
Plan
Capitalized when referring to our second-phase deliverable. Lowercase otherwise.
Pre-remodel advisor
The role title for Ashley and Paige. Hyphenated. Avoid "consultant" — too transactional.
Walk / walkthrough
For VR sessions. "Walk your home" is better than "tour your space."
Construction budget
What homeowners will spend with their contractor. Distinct from our service fee. Never blur the two.
